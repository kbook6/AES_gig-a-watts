How the CIRI Score is built

CIRI is a decision supporter, not a decision maker. It does not set queue position or replace MISO's DPP studies. Each point traces back to a named input.

Critical risk variableHow to earn pointsMax
Development MaturityFull site control for production facilities, gen-tie and POI (12) · community engagement (6) · PPA maturity (8) · capital readiness for M2 and an estimated M3 (9)35
Grid ImpactCrowding: this project plus queued MW vs. the POI's headroom (10) · grid strength rated strong / moderate / likely weak, from nearby synchronous plants and inverter-based projects (8) · thermal headroom for this project alone (6) · distance from MISO seams (6)30
Interconnection FlexibilityCo-located storage (8) · accepts curtailment (6) · flexible load or grid-forming control (6)20
Technical Data & Model QualityPSS/E and PSCAD models ready to submit (8) · detailed SLDs (4) · site plan with coordinates (3)15
Readiness bands and incentives

High readiness 80+ Medium 60–79 Low <60. Every score shows an illustrative ±5 range (to be replaced by backtested uncertainty) and is labeled non-binding and self-reported until MISO verifies it.
Phase 5 incentive: fast-tracked review for verified scores of 85+, after FERC approval.
Storage earns points in Interconnection Flexibility only; in Grid Impact it changes the cost estimate, not the points. Gas and other synchronous generators receive full grid-strength points because they add fault current; this reflects grid physics, not a technology preference.

POI crowding rating

Green when this project plus queued MW stays under 2× the POI's headroom, Yellow up to 4×, Red above. This is the crowding alert: a site flips from Green to Yellow or Red once requests pass a set multiple of its headroom. Crowding carries the most Grid Impact points, because headroom that many projects compete for is not really available to any one of them.

Milestone payments

M2 = $8,000/MW at entry. M3 tops security up to the greater of 20% of the likely network-upgrade cost or $1,000/MW, and M4 tops it up to 30% (never below the M2 already posted). Withdrawal forfeits 10% of M2 at Decision Point I, 35% at Decision Point II, 75% in Phase III and 100% at GIA negotiation. Exit is penalty-free if upgrade costs jump more than 50% (35% at the final study) and $10k/MW.

Dropout risk

Not shown in this prototype. In Phase 1 it is built as a separate model on MISO outcomes (withdrawal before Decision Point II), using only data available on the application date, and backtested before MISO review. It is reported beside the CIRI score and used to check it. For context: only 24% of MISO queue projects are built, and about 70% of requests withdraw.
