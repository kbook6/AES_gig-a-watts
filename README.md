## AES Innovation Challenge     
The AES Innovation Challenge is a prize-based competition for graduate students interested in gaining experience in real-world energy challenges. This year's challenge was to develop a solution in policy, technology and innovation to address a problem within grid interconnection in the United States energy grid.
